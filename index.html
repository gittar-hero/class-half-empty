About & credits

Class Half Empty is an independent student project. It is not an official Technion service and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Technion.

Timetable data comes from the Technion's public course catalog, collected by technion-sap-info-fetcher by Michael Maltsev, the data source behind CheeseFork. Huge thanks to the creators of CheeseFork: this app couldn't exist without their work. Class Half Empty is not part of CheeseFork, and its code is independent.

"Free" means no course in the Technion timetable is scheduled there at that time. Exams, events, reservations and locked rooms aren't in the data, and only rooms that host at least one course this semester are listed. Always check the official Technion timetable before relying on a room.
